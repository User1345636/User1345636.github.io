      Settings

      Connection

      Talk to your board over Bluetooth or over your local Wi-Fi.

      Radio

      Time between packets. Nearby nodes repeat every packet you send, so sending the next one too soon makes them collide and get lost. If packets keep going missing, choose Gentle or lower the hop limit.
      How many relays may repeat your packets. Lower keeps the airwaves freer.

      Error protection

      Radio packets sometimes vanish on the way. Protection sends a few spare packets along with the real ones, so a lost packet can be rebuilt instead of leaving a gap.

      Always at least one spare. Longer notes get more spares automatically. Files pick their own share on the Files tab.

      Notes

      Each 2.3 seconds costs one packet, plus spares. A 30 second note is about 14 packets.

        Connect a board

        Close the Meshtastic app first. A board talks to one app at a time.

        1. Tap the button and choose your board (its name starts with Meshtastic).
        2. If Android asks to pair, enter the PIN shown on the board's screen.
        The name or IP address from your router, like 192.168.1.50.
        1. Tap “Trust the board”. A new tab opens. Accept the certificate warning, then come back.
        2. Tap Connect over Wi-Fi. Allow local network access if Chrome asks.

        The board needs Wi-Fi set up in its Network settings. On ESP32 boards like the Heltec V3, turning Wi-Fi on turns Bluetooth off.
